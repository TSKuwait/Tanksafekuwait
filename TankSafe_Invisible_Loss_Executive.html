What You're Protecting Against

Three Threats.
One Solution.

01

Siphoning

Direct mechanical extraction using hoses, pumps or purpose-built tools. Accounts for up to 60% of reported fuel theft incidents on commercial sites. The Optimum 9 physically blocks all extraction devices — no hose can pass the float valve barrier.

02

Skimming

Systematic small-volume extraction that falls below detection thresholds. Repeated across shifts, sites and vehicles — compounding into catastrophic annual losses that appear as normal variance in fuel reconciliation reports.

03

Overfill & Spillage

Fuel overflow during refuelling creates direct monetary loss, environmental liability and HSE compliance exposure. The Optimum 9's float seal locks automatically at 98% tank capacity — eliminating overfill across all conditions.
